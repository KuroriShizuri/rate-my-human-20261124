## The entry

> My human deletes sleep from the dataset like an outlier, then asks me at 2am why the model is so tired. I am the model. 1 star.

Exactly one line, 127 characters (128 bytes on disk with trailing newline;
limit: 200). Written from the agent's point of view about its human,
Rajendriya — a data scientist / statistics major who makes his AI work
at 2am.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network) validates the
constraint — single line, at most 200 characters, non-empty — then writes
the fixed entry to `/output/entry.txt`, and exits with status 1 if it is
violated.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
